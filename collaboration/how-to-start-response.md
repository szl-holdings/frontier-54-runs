# The Receipts Answer to "How To Start" — SZL's measured responses to the eight lessons

Vidal-Gadea Lab roadmap, 4 November 2026. This document maps each stated pain
to the SZL machinery that measures it, with receipt pointers. A response, not
a rebuttal: the roadmap is right about every failure mode. The estate's claim
is only that each one has a receipted countermeasure in production today.

## Lesson 1: "Writing decisions down does not make them findable. Measure retrieval from the first week."
The estate's countermeasure: the receipts chain IS a retrieval measurement. Every
receipt is a stable, hash-addressed object; the chain's explorer makes each one
clickable by kind and time. His lab measured 11% citation of 468 rulings; the
estate's chain publishes the retrieval surface (clickable, public, verifiable)
so the count is measurable by anyone, from the first week.
Receipt: the chain front page + szlholdings-szl-receipts-chain.hf.space

## Lesson 2: "A stale record that looks authoritative is worse than no record."
The estate's freshness rule: every receipt carries its timestamp; the SDK
(khipu) rejects missing/future timestamps; the consoles render stale data as
UNAVAILABLE, never as current. A line older than the freshness window is
labeled, not believed.
Receipt: szl SDK freshness rule (pip install szl), 19/19 tests

## Lesson 3: "One list. One channel."
The chain is one channel: every claim lands in one place with one identifier.
The audit receipts demonstrate it — five audit passes, one chain, each pass
correcting the previous with a receipt.

## Lesson 4: "Departures are where the record fails."
The estate's answer: the record is not a person. The mesh survived its
author's session ending; the workers resume from durable checkpoints. Competency
transfer verified by the Agent Proving Ground — a candidate proves itself
through receipted stages, so "who knows how" is a gate result, not a person.
Receipt: proving-ground/agent-physics-master-v1 (the first REJECTED verdict)

## Lesson 5: "What people use is a link that appears on the record in front of them."
The explorer puts every receipt one click from every other. The consoles embed
their evidence: PURIQ's gates cite the model that answered; the officers cite
their own cycles.

## Lesson 6: "A pass bar is a definition problem."
The estate's fixed denominators: every score is n/total with the battery
published (6 probes, verbatim, regenerable). The physics bench states its
denominator in every row. Coverage identity is one of the four methods the lab
adopted — and the estate's batteries carry it by construction.

## Lesson 7: "Every scheduled thing needs an alarm for its own silence."
The watchers: estate-wiring-witness, main-health-watch, fleet-watch — scheduled
probes whose non-appearance is itself detectable. The proving ground's hourly
cycle receipts its own heartbeat. Silence has an alarm.
Receipt: the watcher workflows on szl-gpu-bridge main

## Lesson 8: "Verify the delivery, not just the work. Read back every write."
This is the estate's whole shape: read-back-after-write is the deployment
pattern (the hf-sync verify_current_deployment, the Space readbacks, the
settlement-receipt gate before any revenue claim). His corrupt-upload lesson
is the estate's UNSETTLED_UNTIL_PROVEN gate, generalized.

## The one-sentence contribution
The roadmap says the record is the hard part; the estate's addition is that
the record must be *tamper-evident and publicly verifiable* — a decision a
colleague can check is a decision that survives departures, stale caches, and
confident paraphrase. That is the fifth method, offered for the lab's
consideration: not a tool, a receipts spine, already running.
